PJ IS 32x32 SO ALL ART PIECES NEED TO SCALE WITH PJ AND CAMERA ZOOM IN MIND (DONT WANT BIG THINGS TO APPEAR OFFSCREEN NOW DO YOU) (IF U WANT THE SPECIFICS GO INTO GODOT AND TEST THINGS OUT YOURSELF LOL #REKT)

AND IF ITS A CHARACTER OR LIKE A PROJECTILE AND STUFF IT NEEDS AN OUTLINE TO STAND OUT FROM THE BG
MAKE SURE THE ARTSTYLE FITS WITH THE THEME OF THE LEVEL YOURE WORKING ON

CONSIDER COLOR PALETTES WHEN DESIGNING TO ENSURE THAT THINGS THAT NEED TO STAND OUT STAND OUT
FOR EXAMPLE, PJ IS LIKE WHITE AND GOLD SO I MADE HIS SURFBOARD A WARM COLOR OF GOLD/ORANGE/RED. THE USAGE OF SIMILAR COLORS FOR A WHOLE CHARACTER MAKES IT EASIER TO DESIGN A BACKGROUND THAT MAKES ALL THE DETAILS OF THE CHARACTER POP OUT. IF YOU MAKE A CHARACTER WITH LIKE RED/BLUE/GREEN/GASTER ITLL BE HARDER TO PICK A COLOR THAT THE CHARACTER WILL STAND OUT WELL
